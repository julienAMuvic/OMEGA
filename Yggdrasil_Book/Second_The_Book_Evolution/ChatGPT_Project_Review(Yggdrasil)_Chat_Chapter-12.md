1) Why Post-Human Inclusion Matters
-----------------------------------

Engineered bioforms and hive entities are no longer speculative. Wetware labs can mint bespoke lineages; clades adapt to extreme habitats; consensus collectives coordinate faster than firms. Without a compact, three risks compound: biosafety failures, coercive assimilation, and governance capture. The Rails are the minimal law of peace that make pluralism practical: **Exit & Safe Passage; Non-Hostility & Non-Dispossession; Rescue First; Neutral Courts; Open Corridors & Standards (≥3 implementations); Leashed Replication.** The Five Pillars—**Birth/Crew; Language/Codec; Land/Habitat; Skill/Reliability; Security (bounded)**—define what must be raised together. Inclusion is not indulgence. It is a bet on redundancy, creativity, and bounded security. Diversity of form is security when it is tethered to shared rails, transparent remedies, and lineages that can be reversed or slowed. That is what follows.

2) Taxonomy & Scope
-------------------

**Bioforms.** Engineered biological lineages and forks, including: edited human and near-human lineages; parthenogenetic cohorts; symbiotic or chimeric clades; gene-drive organisms; ex vivo organoid cooperatives; and bio-cybernetically augmented crews where the biological substrate remains decisive for identity.

**Hives.** Consensus entities formed by many persons or sub-persons with shared state and coordinated action. “Tight” hives run high-cohesion consensus (low latency, strong binding); “loose” hives run low-cohesion consensus (higher latency, shallow binding). Hives may be wet, dry, or hybrid.

**Out of scope.** Purely synthetic Tier-4 minds are treated in **Ch. 11: AI, Tier-4 Synthetics & Cybernetics**. When a hybrid crosses that threshold, apply that chapter in addition to the rails here.

**Key terms (used throughout).**

*   **Consent Ledger.** An append-only, auditable record of binding, fission, merge, and unbinding consents, with **cool-off windows** and revocation paths.
*   **Individuation.** The right and practical ability for a node or sub-node to unbind or leave, with **safe-harbor exit** corridors and anti-retaliation sanctions.
*   **Gene-Drive Gate.** A licensed, two-key, reversibility-proven release mechanism for gene drives and counter-drives (escrowed).
*   **Interface Budget.** Rate/bandwidth caps and reservations that prevent any collective from flooding a shared rail.
*   **Replicative Prudence (RGP).** Signed lineages, rate limits, reversibility proofs, and bond schedules that enforce **G×R ≤ 0.3×p×S×L** (Attacker Gain × Repeatability vs Detection × Sanction × Latency; see **Ch. 9: Replication Governance & Lineage**).

3) Shared Compact — The Post-Human Bill of Rails
------------------------------------------------

**Rights (substrate-neutral).**

1.  **Exit & Individuation.** Any consenting node may unbind and depart via guaranteed corridors, with rescue coverage and price-capped transport.
2.  **Non-Hostility & Non-Dispossession.** No seizure or sanction for non-membership; no collective punishment across a clade or hive.
3.  **Rescue First.** Search-and-rescue minima apply regardless of morphology or group status; triage follows capability and need, not politics.
4.  **Neutral Courts.** Bond-backed tribunals provide **Interim Orders ≤72h** and **Final Orders ≤14–30d**, with public reasons and docketed evidence bundles.
5.  **Open Corridors & Standards.** Critical interfaces use open, conformance-tested standards with **≥3 independent implementations** and rotating editors (see **Ch. 7: Standards as Commons**).
6.  **Leashed Replication.** Wet and dry replication obey rate limits, signed lineages, and reversibility or quarantine plans; overrides require two keys and public logging.

**Duties (substrate-neutral).**

1.  **Consent Integrity.** Maintain consent ledgers with verifiable identities and cool-off windows; attest to informed participation and alarm thresholds.
2.  **Lineage Honesty.** Sign releases, forward telemetry, and maintain **Golden Chain of Custody** for samples and logs (see **Ch. 6: Atrocity-Hardened Fallback**).
3.  **Interface Discipline.** Respect interface budgets; buy capacity or queue; no denial-of-service against shared rails.
4.  **Remedy Compliance.** Honor tribunal orders promptly; accept **sanctions before shots**—port/slot denial, bond burns, schedule changes—prior to any physical enforcement.
5.  **No Singleton Control.** Do not capture a public rail; any emergency centralization must be a **CUCC Cage**: two-key, narrow scope, hard sunsets, quorum engines, dated re-federalization.

4) Bioforms — Governance & Dignity
----------------------------------

**Genetic Dignity & Consent.** No forced edits, sterilization, or reproductive suppression without a tribunal order meeting strict necessity and proportionality, with a **sunset** and independent medical ombud present. For minors or dependent clades, use guardianship with “best-interest plus emergent-autonomy” standards and periodic review.

**Gene-Drive Gate.** Any gene drive (public health, agriculture, habitat remediation) passes through a **Gate**: license issuance requires (a) signed lineage, (b) reversibility proof (counter-drive or functional brake), (c) monitoring plan with sample cadence, (d) incident escrow that can trigger a rollback.

**Metabolic Rights.** **CB (Civic Base)** guarantees clade-specific nutritional packs and meds at price caps during corridor disruptions, with inventory bonds and replenishment SLAs. For exotic diets, ports maintain substitution ladders and emergency synthesis windows.

5) Hives — Consent, Individuation, Interface Budgets
----------------------------------------------------

**Consent Ledgers.** Binding into a hive requires a recorded consent with: scope, terms, alarm thresholds, exit costs (if any), and a **cool-off window**. Merges and fissions require reciprocal postings to a public docket and notice to affected corridors.

**Right to Individuate.** Any node may initiate unbinding. Corridors guarantee safe exit with **non-retaliation sanctions**: if the hive obstructs, tribunals deny ports/slots and burn bonds until restitution. **Rescue First** covers nodes during contested exits.

*   **Case B (field).** In a desert region, a tight hive attempts to block a minority fission. The **Consent Ledger** shows a valid individuation intent. A tribunal issues an **Interim Order**: safe-harbor corridors open; three individuals unbind during the cool-off. Retaliatory denial of tools triggers schedule denial and bond burns. The hive survives—and reforms governance thresholds.

**Merge/Fission Treaty.** Set quorum thresholds (e.g., ⅔ for merge, ⅗ for fission), notice periods, and asset treatment (use escrow to prevent grabs). Publish a post-event audit of consent integrity.

**Interface Budgeting.** Hives negotiate **I/O budgets** with corridor operators: per-interval message caps, reservation windows, and priority lanes for life safety. Violations degrade quality of service automatically; repeated breaches escalate to port denial.

**Representation Rule.** Representation scales with verified individuation and audited corridor impact, not raw node count; caps prevent governance capture. Loose hives may aggregate for discussion; votes allocate by audited membership with anti-sybil proofs.

9) Fallback & War — Keeping Rails Under Extreme Stress
------------------------------------------------------

Crises tempt empire. **Atrocity-Hardened Fallback (AHF)** exists to resist that pull. It uses **multi-signal triggers**, **logs before labels**, **protected green lanes**, **narrow Interim Orders**, and **loud sunsets** that force review.

**Spatially Exact Quarantines.** Quarantine by module and corridor segment, not by clade identity. A gene-drive spill should trigger a **rollback lane** and sample embargo by lot numbers, not population sweeps.

**Module-Targeted Dampers.** In a hive incursion, damp I/O at the interface—not at the person. Rate-limit collective channels; preserve personal exit, counsel, and ombud ramps.

**Two-Key Decapitation, Not Collective Punishment.** If a hive’s core coordinator is hijacked, a **CUCC Cage** can sever it for **7/30/90 days**—two keys, narrow scope, quorum engines. During the interval, **exfil lanes** remain open for unbinding nodes. The cage’s sole purpose is to **reopen the rails**, followed by a dated **re-federalization** plan.

**Golden Chain of Custody.** All evidence (telemetry, samples, logs) is hashed, time-boxed, and stored across quorum vaults. Tribunals review **evidence bundles** quickly, with **public reasons**, so the public sees discipline, not vengeance.

**War Doctrine Without Empire.** In conflict, the goal is to **fight to reopen rails, not to rule them** (see **Ch. 10**). That means ports, slots, and bond-priced sanctions first; targeted force only to restore corridors, rescue lanes, and neutral courts.

5) Hives: Consent, Individuation, and Interfaces

**Consent Ledgers**

Hives often communicate with high frequency; shared rails cannot be a hostage. Interface budgets define rate caps, reservation windows, and priority classes for life safety and rollback. Loose hives may aggregate for discussion; voting ties to verified membership and corridor contribution. The aim is simple: give voice proportionate to verifiable weight without inviting sybil floods.
