1) Why Dyson Stewardship (from extraction to husbandry)
-------------------------------------------------------

“Dyson” here is not conquest or a single megastructure; it is the slow, careful husbandry of light. The work is civil, not imperial: building corridors, budgets, and habits that keep many forms alive around a star without collapsing into monopoly or militarization.

Continuity Modulation (CM) is the operating system for that work. The **Five Pillars**—**Birth/Crew Formation**, **Language/Codec**, **Land/Habitat**, **Skill/Reliability**, **Security (bounded)**—anchor what must rise together. The **Rails**—**Exit & Safe Passage**, **Non-Hostility & Non-Dispossession**, **Rescue First**, **Neutral Courts**, **Open Corridors & Standards (≥3 implementations)**, **Leashed Replication**—are the minimal law of peace at stellar scale.

Stewardship means: publish uncertainty; price defects; prefer **sanctions-before-shots**; keep **no singletons** anywhere near public rails. The result is not a neat empire but a reliable commons that tolerates difference and failure (see “Sanctions Before Shots”; see “Rails Overview”).

* * *

2) Thermodynamic Constraints & the Maintenance-First Budget
-----------------------------------------------------------

Waste heat is the arithmetic you cannot negotiate away. Every watt captured or redirected must leave as photons. Radiators are not “nice-to-have”; they are the limiting reagent.

**Maintenance-first budgeting** starts with cooling, not construction. Every new array, beam, or fab must post an ex ante **Radiator Endowment** sized to (a) peak thermal load at **P95**, (b) refurbishment outages, and (c) incident surge. Endowments are held in a **Cooling AMM** (automated market maker) that allocates “cooling credits” hour by hour and enforces **circuit breakers** when volatility threatens stability.

Cooling behaves like a scarce commodity with integrity constraints. We use fees and schedule denial—**sanctions-before-shots**—to correct abuse. Forced is only to **reopen the rails**, and only after bonded tribunals rule (see “Neutral Courts & Tribunals”).

Key numbers:

*   **Endowment floor:** ≥ **45 days** of P95 thermal load per major node.
*   **Refurbishment cadence:** **1/30** of radiator area offline at any time; rolling refurbishment window ≤ **18 months**.
*   **Surge buffer:** emergency **green-lane** cooling capacity ≥ **15%** of star-side demand, released by posted rules.
*   **Telemetry interval:** ≤ **60 seconds** updates to public cooling ledger.

* * *

3) The SB/ISB Layers (Star & Interstellar Rails)
------------------------------------------------

We name two baseline layers:

*   **SB (Star Baseline) rails**: cislunar-to-outer system corridors, power-beam slots, docking and traffic, maintenance calendars, cooling markets, and neutral courts reachable with ≤ **30-minute** signaling delay.
*   **ISB (Interstellar Baseline) rails**: beam etiquette beyond a star’s Hill sphere, **SAR precedence** over commercial traffic, biosphere restraint, and tribunal process adapted to **light-lag**.

Minimal enforceables across both:

1.  **Exit & Safe Passage**—posted corridors with **SLA ≥ 99.97% monthly availability**, grace windows during maintenance, and detours when corridors narrow.
2.  **Neutral Courts & Tribunals**—bond-backed, with **Interim Orders (IO) ≤ 72 hours**, **Final Orders (FO) ≤ 14–30 days**, posted reasons, and mirrored evidence.
3.  **Rescue First**—SAR lanes that preempt commercial slots; **dispatch ≤ 15 minutes** (SB) and **positive control** acknowledgments within **one light-lag cycle** (ISB).
4.  **Open Standards (≥3 implementations)**—Dock/Beam/Sched/Traffic stacks each certified on open conformance suites; no single vendor can gatekeep.
5.  **Leashed Replication**—signed lineages, rate limits, teardown lotteries, and bond-priced externalities for human, biological, cybernetic, and synthetic replication (see “Replication Governance & Lineage”).

* * *

4) Radiator Economy & Cooling Credits
-------------------------------------

Treat cooling as money you can’t counterfeit. **Cooling credits** denominate transferable rights to emit heat into posted radiative capacity windows. Credits reside in a **Cooling AMM** that clears hourly based on available radiator area, angle, emissivity, and background temperature.

Core design:

*   **Endowments:** Every operator posts a **Cooling Endowment ≥ 45-day P95**. The AMM may lend against endowments with **LTV ≤ 60%**, auto-liquidating via **price-capped auctions** when buffers fall.
*   **Price Caps & Circuit Breakers:**
    *   **Cap 1 (spread):** If the bid–ask spread exceeds **4× 30-day median**, trading halts **5 minutes**.
    *   **Cap 2 (volatility-of-volatility):** If 5-minute realized vol exceeds **3×** rolling 24h vol, the AMM widens tick sizes and raises margin requirements **2×**.
    *   **Cap 3 (temperature):** If average radiator temperature exceeds **P99–2σ** for **10 minutes**, the AMM triggers **cooldown auctions** drawing from strategic buffers; **new heat intake throttled −20%** for **2 hours**.
*   **Position Limits:** Single actor cannot hold > **15%** of credits in any **6-hour** window; pooled affiliates aggregated.
*   **Commit–Reveal Maintenance:** Operators commit hashed maintenance windows **T−30 days**, reveal parameters **T−7 days**. Neutral monitors flag clusters; **elasticity floor ≥ 1.2** must be met during maintenance, or bonds burn.

**Sanctions-before-shots**: violations trigger **ports/slots denial** and **bond burns proportional to thermal risk**, not police action. If a cartel squeezes cooling, **tribunals** can order **forced credit release** from endowments within **72 hours** to **reopen the rails**.

5) Replication Governance (RGP) in Space
----------------------------------------

Replication grows capacity—and risk—exponentially. **Leashed Replication** means permissioned growth with audit trails.

Rules:

*   **Signed Lineages:** Every replicator, fab line, and bio-culture carries a **cryptographic lineage** (hash chain) back to root approvals, with **Golden Chain of Custody** mirrored to neutral escrow weekly.
*   **Throttle Profile:** Each class has a posted rate limit (e.g., **≤ 1.35×/30 days** expansion), variances allowed only with **bonded exception** and cooling proof.
*   **Teardown Lotteries:** **1%–3%** of deployed units are randomly selected quarterly for teardown audits; failure rates > **0.7%** trigger **widened sampling** and **bond surcharges** next quarter.
*   **Import Bonds:** Imports into SB/ISB corridors require **defect bonds** sized to **0.3× p × S × L** (probability × sanction × latency) to satisfy the adversary test; burns fund recall and SAR.
*   **Parity Across Substrates:** Biological and synthetic replication follow the same **lineage, throttle, teardown, and bond** rules.

**Sanctions-before-shots**: noncompliance means **schedule denial**, **port access revocation**, and **lineage quarantine**; force is used only to neutralize active harm and **reopen the rails** (see “AHF”).

6) Beamed Power & Slot Etiquette (Traffic/Beam/Sched)
-----------------------------------------------------

Beams are corridors with physics attached. Miss the envelope and you blind a sensor, boil a panel, or worse.

Standards:

*   **Multi-Implementation Schedulers:** At least **three** independent schedulers (different codebases and teams) run in **quorum engines**; **failover ≤ 90 seconds**, **MTTR ≤ 6 hours**.
*   **Safety Envelopes:** Beam roll-off and side-lobe leakage must stay within posted **mW/cm²** limits at protected zones; **independent monitors** cross-check with sky surveys.
*   **Slot Etiquette:**
    *   Frequency & beam windows posted **14 days** ahead;
    *   **Grace window** ≥ **90 seconds** on handover;
    *   **Dark-idle** safe-fail within **3 minutes** after control loss;
    *   **SAR precedence**: preempt any commercial slot within **≤ 2 minutes** of authenticated distress.
*   **Conformance:** Public suites simulate jitter, ephemeris error, and scheduler disagreement. **Pass/fail** matrices are posted; any stack failing **two consecutive rounds** loses corridor priority.

**Sanctions-before-shots**: Violators face **slot denial** and **bond burns**; force only to stop ongoing damage or re-open SAR corridors (see “Rescue First”).

7) Interstellar SAR (Rescue First at Light-Lag)
-----------------------------------------------

Rescue-first must survive latency. **ISB SAR** makes this concrete:

*   **Neutral Hubs:** Pre-positioned depots with fuel, coolant, shelters, and repair kits every **0.1 light-years** along active corridors.
*   **Authenticated Distress:** Dual-channel distress (RF + optical), **one-time pads** pre-shared at departure; **positive control** requires at least **one round-trip acknowledgment** when feasible.
