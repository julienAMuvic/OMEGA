-------------------------------

Atrocities are designed to break judgment. They weaponize grief, purity instincts, and outrage cycles to push communities into collective punishment and permanent command. The aim is not only to harm bodies but to melt the Rails. If we abandon exit and safe passage, neutral courts, multi-implementation standards, and leashed replication in the first 72 hours, we give attackers the prize they cannot win by force: a frightened public that consents to indefinite centralization. AHF exists to block that arc.

This chapter is an operator’s and policy guide to act decisively without dissolving ethics or entrenching the CUCC Cage. It also provides playbooks for four hard patterns: corridor massacre, SAR ambush, bio/eco attack, and staged “rogue AI” theater.

Rails That Must Survive (Non-Derogables)
----------------------------------------

During crisis, the Rails are not optional—they are the operating surface:

*   **Exit & Safe Passage:** Corridors remain open; postings and SLAs are honored.
*   **Non-Hostility & Non-Dispossession:** No violence against non-members; no seizure without individualized orders and logs.

*   **Leashed Replication:** Signed lineages, rate limits, two-key overrides, and bond-priced externalities prevent panic bans and scapegoats.

Tie them to the **Five Pillars**: Birth/Crew (protect families/crews during evacuation), Language/Codec (clear signals and receipts), Land/Habitat (keep utilities online), Skill/Reliability (trained operators with checklists), Security (bounded; sanctions before shots). The **Pillars** set what must be raised; the **Rails** set how we move.

Trigger Discipline: Multi-Signal + Petition Bonds
-------------------------------------------------

AHF activates only when three conditions are tested against the 2-of-3 rule:

1.  **Objective Metric Breach** (e.g., mass-casualty threshold, corridor kill-zone detection, pathogen anomaly).
2.  **Independent Observer** (certified third party, not in the Commander’s chain).
3.  **Cryptographically Signed Evidence Bundle** (hashes, time, location, lineage).

This aligns incentives without delaying response: the trigger checklists are pre-staged, and ingest to the evidence vault begins at T+0.

This is the CUCC Cage doctrine applied narrowly: exceptional command in a box, quorum engines under the hood, dated **re-federalization** pre-published, and no singletons on any control plane.

-----------------------------------------------------

“Proofs, not promises” is the only antidote to atrocity propaganda. The **Golden Chain of Custody** is a neutral evidence vault with:

*   **Signed Logs:** Every ingest has a cryptographic hash, time, location, and collector identity.

*   **Privacy Budget:** Pre-set caps on personally identifying detail per day; auditors can request elevated access under sealed interim orders.
