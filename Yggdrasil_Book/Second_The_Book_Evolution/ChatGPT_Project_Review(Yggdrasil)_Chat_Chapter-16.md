The task is not to surrender; it is to finish well—keeping the rails open, the pillars upright, and the door unlocked for those who come after. Why Endings Matter
------------------

Endings shape meaning. Without planned endings, systems decay into drift—permissions stay open, archives rot silently, and emergency powers become normal. Planning dignified decline is a civic duty. It honors the living by preserving options and lowers tail risks for the next crew.

Continuity Modulation is not expansionist optimism; it is reliability realism. Late eras demand rebalancing, not abdication.

Thermodynamics Meets Governance
-------------------------------

Thinning negentropy changes governance tempos. Cooling windows mean:

*   **Fewer turns of the wrench**: schedules spread out; preventive maintenance beats hot fixes.
*   **Longer adjudication tails**: we must still hit tribunal SLAs—**Interim ≤72h; Final ≤14–30d**—but do so with slower, more deliberate cycles and reversible commitments.
*   **Budget inversion**: maintenance-first budgets become law. Capital growth projects require sunset bonds and back-out plans; ops budgets carry refurbishment endowments by default.

*   Publish **Energy Cadence Profiles** for each public rail (see Methods Annex).

Reversible Computing & Slow Clocks
----------------------------------

Compute must move from frenetic to frugal. Reversible or near-reversible logic, aggressive checkpointing, and sparse scheduling cut entropy budgets. This is not an all-at-once refit; it is a rolling migration.

Principles:

*   **Reversible-first where feasible**: logic families and algorithms selected for low dissipation; where not, adopt **sparse duty cycles** and compressive audit trails.

*   **Fixity over flash**: prioritize integrity checks, lineage signatures, and deterministic replays over peak throughput.
