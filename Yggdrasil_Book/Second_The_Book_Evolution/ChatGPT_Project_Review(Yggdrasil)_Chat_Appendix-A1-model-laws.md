# A1.1 — Confederated Baselines (CB) Charter Act

## Article 1 — Purpose

1.1 Establish minimal, enforceable baselines (“Rails”) enabling plural local charters to coexist under a thin law of peace.
1.2 Protect exit and safe passage, non-hostility and non-dispossession, rescue-first obligations, neutral adjudication, open corridors and standards, and responsible replication.

## Article 2 — Definitions

2.1 **Local Sovereignty Charter (LSC):** A posted constitution of a community, association, or polity.
2.2 **Corridor:** Any designated route, port, slot, beam, or interface enabling movement, trade, or access.
2.3 **CB Minima:** Baseline guarantees in Articles 3–5.
2.4 **Tribunal:** Neutral adjudicator accredited under Article 6.
2.5 **Emergency (Fallback):** Time-boxed powers invoked under Article 7.
2.6 **Replication:** Creation or forking of factories, tooling, agents, or lineages (wet or dry).

## Article 3 — Exit & Safe Passage

3.1 Every person retains the right to depart an LSC with their property.
3.2 Corridors must be posted publicly with price ceilings and capacities.
3.3 Denial of exit is prohibited except per Tribunal Interim Order (IO) not exceeding 72 hours.

## Article 4 — Non-Hostility & Non-Dispossession

4.1 No LSC may dispossess non-members of essentials (water, air, power, medical triage, basic food, safe shelter).
4.2 Collective punishments are prohibited.

## Article 5 — Rescue First & Neutral Minima

5.1 Search-and-Rescue (SAR) and emergency aid precede litigation.
5.2 Triage hubs must serve without discrimination, subject to safety protocols.
5.3 Costs are allocable post hoc by Tribunal Final Order (FO).

## Article 6 — Neutral Courts & Tribunals

6.1 Accredited Tribunals must meet SLAs: IO ≤ 72 hours; FO ≤ 14 days (red card) / ≤ 30 days (yellow).
6.2 Reasons and evidence summaries shall be published with privacy safeguards.

## Article 7 — Open Corridors & Standards

7.1 Critical rails (Dock/Beam/Sched/Traffic/Comms) shall have ≥ 3 independently developed, certified implementations.
7.2 Conformance suites shall be open, with 12-month grace windows for upgrades.

## Article 8 — Leashed Replication

8.1 Replication requires signed lineage, rate limits, bonds, and teardown audit randomization per the Replication Code.

## Article 9 — Governance

9.1 CB Authority accredits Tribunals, registers corridors, and publishes conformance results.
9.2 CB Authority may not legislate LSC internals beyond CB Minima.

## Article 10 — Enforcement & Sanctions

10.1 Primary tools: bond burns, port/slot denial, fee uplifts, and corridor access throttles.
10.2 Force is limited to protection of life, green lanes, and immediate threats, with black-box logs.

## Article 11 — Sunset & Review

11.1 CB provisions are permanent; emergency exceptions sunset per Article 7 of A1.4.
11.2 Biennial public review of SLAs and corridor price ceilings.

# A1.2 — Corridor Service Level Agreement (SLA) Statute

## Article 1 — Purpose

1.1 Guarantee predictable, fair access to corridors (routes/ports/slots/beams/interfaces) supporting exit, trade, SAR, and essential services.

## Article 2 — Definitions

2.1 **Elasticity Floor:** Minimum surge capacity above 95th percentile load.
2.2 **Price Cap:** Max fee per unit (time/throughput/berth).
2.3 **Commit–Reveal Schedule:** Cryptographic pre-commitment of maintenance windows, revealed ≥ 14 days prior.

## Article 3 — Requirements

3.1 Capacity: maintain elasticity ≥ 20% over 95th percentile hourly demand.
3.2 Price: cap at median rolling 90-day cost + 15%; no surge pricing beyond cap.
3.3 Maintenance: commit–reveal; overlapping critical corridors prohibited unless Tribunal-approved.
3.4 Green Lanes: SAR/medical/essentials reserved ≥ 5% capacity at zero or regulated cost.
3.5 Transparency: publish real-time capacity, queue times, and outages.

## Article 4 — Governance

4.1 CB Corridor Office verifies schedules, capacity, and caps.
4.2 Annual third-party audit of elasticity and pricing accuracy.

## Article 5 — Enforcement & Sanctions

5.1 Breach triggers fee rebate (150% of overcharge) and slot denial for repeat violations (2 strikes/180 days).
5.2 Cartel behavior (price/maintenance coordination) → joint red card: trustee control 90 days.

## Article 6 — Sunset & Review

6.1 Statute stands; individual maintenance exceptions auto-expire at window end.
6.2 Semiannual review of elasticity and caps.

# A1.3 — Tribunal Service Levels & Procedure Act

## Article 1 — Purpose

1.1 Ensure fast, fair, transparent adjudication supporting sanctions-before-force.

## Article 2 — Definitions

2.1 **Interim Order (IO):** Reversible order pending final adjudication.
2.2 **Final Order (FO):** Determination on merits.
2.3 **Red/Yellow Cards:** Severity classes mapping to FO SLAs.

## Article 3 — SLAs

3.1 IO issuance within ≤ 72 hours of complete filing in urgent rail cases; ≤ 7 days otherwise.
3.2 FO: red card ≤ 14 days; yellow card ≤ 30 days.
3.3 Publication of reasons and evidence summaries within 72 hours of IO/FO.

## Article 4 — Procedure

4.1 Filing Stake refundable upon prevailing or precedent-setting contribution.
4.2 Summary judgment lane for patterned disputes (target ≤ 7 days).
4.3 Evidence handling: Golden Chain of Custody; privacy-respecting disclosure.
4.4 Appeals affect damages, not immediate containment, except manifest error.

## Article 5 — Governance

5.1 Accreditation standards; ombuds rotation; reproducibility bounties for public datasets and code.

## Article 6 — Enforcement & Sanctions

6.1 Orders bind corridor/port operators; non-compliance → bond burns and slot denial.
6.2 Frivolous litigation → stake forfeiture and filing rate limits.

## Article 7 — Sunset & Review

7.1 IOs auto-expire at FO or 30 days, whichever earlier.
7.2 Quarterly SLA audit; publish IO/FO latency medians.
